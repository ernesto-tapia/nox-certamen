# Nox Certamen Combat Quick Reference

1. **Declare:** Up to two origins; leave one troop in each. Adjacent origins may combine against one target.
2. **React:** Defender may trigger its hidden Reaction.
3. **Build pools:** Troops + bonuses, maximum 8d6 per side. Damage removes defense bonus dice first.
4. **Roll and sort:** Highest to lowest; compare matching dice.
5. **Resolve:** Higher die removes one opposing troop. Tie strips a defense bonus and adds damage; with no bonus, defender wins ties.
6. **Occupy or return:** Capture only if every defender falls. Survivors enter on capture or return after failure.

**Repair:** Green 1 · Yellow 2 · Red all. Damage persists across rounds and capture.

**Victory:** Capture every rival HQ, or reach 10 VP.
