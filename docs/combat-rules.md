# Combat Rules — Prototype 0.1

## Attack timing

When an Attack Sequence card resolves, its player may issue up to two orders from distinct owned origins. Each origin must leave one troop behind and must target an adjacent enemy or neutral territory. Targets are chosen at resolution. If a chosen order becomes illegal, the card falls back to fortifying a border territory.

Two origins that target the same territory combine their committed troops into a single battle. Their surviving committed troops occupy together if they capture it.

## Dice pools

Both sides use standard d6 dice and cap their final pool at eight dice.

- Attacker: committed troops, card power, Training Grounds, faction effects, technology, and Reaction bonuses.
- Defender: defending troops multiplied by printed territory defense, then faction, revealed role, fortification, technology, and Reaction bonuses.
- Existing defense-damage markers remove non-troop defensive bonus dice, never troop dice.

Roll once. Sort both pools from highest to lowest and compare paired dice.

- Higher attacker die removes one defending troop.
- Higher defender die removes one committed attacker.
- A tie strips one active non-troop defense die and adds one persistent defense-damage marker; neither side loses a troop.
- If no defensive bonus remains, the defender wins subsequent ties.
- Unpaired dice have no effect.

If all defenders are removed and at least one committed attacker survives, the territory is captured and every surviving committed attacker enters it. Otherwise, survivors return to their origins.

## Infrastructure and HQs

The defending territory's role is revealed when attacked and applies immediately. Captured infrastructure is discarded. Defense damage remains on the territory after capture.

Capturing an HQ eliminates its owner and awards 3 VP. Their remaining infrastructure is discarded, units are removed, and territories become neutral with one troop. Eliminating every rival is an immediate win.

The campaign ends after round 24 if neither victory condition is met. Highest VP wins the tiebreaker, followed by territories and then surviving troops.

## Repair

Repair targets must be owned and connected through owned territories to an Industrial Complex.

- Green Repair: remove 1 marker.
- Yellow Repair: remove up to 2 markers from one territory.
- Red Repair: remove all markers from one territory.

Repair cards with no legal damaged target fall back to Hold.

## Worked example

Five attackers from Vesper Gate and three from Mournwatch combine against four troops in Cairn Hollow. The attacker has one technology bonus: its pool reaches the eight-die cap. Cairn Hollow has defense 2 and a Fortress, so its pool also reaches eight dice. After sorting, the attacker wins three pairs, the defender wins two, and three pairs tie. Three defenders and two attackers are removed; three defense bonuses are stripped and marked as persistent damage. One defender survives, so all surviving attackers return to their origins.
